Leaf & Tale

Privacy Policy

Your journal stays yours.

Effective November 22, 2026

Leaf and Tale is a local-first plant journal. It does not require an account, and we do not operate a server that receives your journal.

What we store

Plants, care events, notes, photo metadata, selected plant photos, settings, backup snapshots, and widget data are stored in the app's private container and its App Group container on your device.

Photos selected in the app are processed into smaller copies for the journal. The originals in your photo library are not modified.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves the device automatically. There is no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, account system, cloud sync, or third-party tracking.

Data leaves the app only when you explicitly:

  • share or save a generated image;
  • export a backup;
  • share or print a yearbook PDF; or
  • send diagnostic files to support.

Apple processes Leaf and Tale Plus purchases and may provide aggregate App Store analytics under Apple's own privacy terms.

Permissions

  • Photos: read only the photos you select.
  • Photo Library Add: save images only when you request it.
  • Camera: take a plant photo only when you request it.
  • Notifications: show the local watering reminder you configure.

Retention and deletion

Journal data remains on your device until you delete plants or photos, reset the app during development, or uninstall the app. Automatic metadata snapshots keep the newest seven active days. User-created backup files are controlled by you.

Tracking and sale of data

Leaf and Tale does not track you across apps or websites, sell personal data, or use personal data for advertising.

Contact

Visit the Leaf and Tale support page for help with the app or privacy questions.
