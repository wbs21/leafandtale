Support

How can we help?

Leaf and Tale requires iOS 17 or later.

Restore Leaf and Tale Plus

Open Settings inside the app, choose the Plus option, then tap Restore purchases. The one-time purchase is tied to the Apple Account used to buy it.

The widget is empty

Open Leaf and Tale once after installation or restart, then add the widget again. The widget reads the same App Group journal as the app.

Back up or move a journal

Open Settings, choose Create backup, and save the ZIP file somewhere safe. On another installation, choose Import backup.

Imports are merged by record ID. Existing local records are preserved when IDs conflict, and repeating the same import does not create duplicates.

Photos or camera are unavailable

Enable Photos or Camera access in iOS Settings for Leaf and Tale. The app reads only the images you select and saves images only when you request it.

Notifications are delayed

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Leaf and Tale. The app sends one aggregate local reminder rather than one notification per plant.

Data privacy

Read the Privacy Policy. The journal stays on-device; there is no account, cloud sync, analytics SDK, or tracking.

Contact

Use the App Support link on the Leaf and Tale App Store listing. Include your iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Do not attach your full journal unless support specifically requests it.
