Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does Leaf and Tale require an account?

No. Open the app and begin. There is no sign-in, cloud account, or server-side journal.

Does my plant data sync to the cloud?

No. Your journal stays on your device. You can create a local ZIP backup and move it wherever you choose.

What does Leaf and Tale Plus include?

Plus is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited plants, watermark-free share cards, the sticker studio, and the PDF yearbook. It is not a subscription.

Is a subscription required?

No. Core journaling and care features are free, and Plus is a single non-consumable purchase processed by Apple.

Can I use Leaf and Tale on iPad?

Yes. The app is built for iPhone and iPad, with adaptive layouts and local widgets.
